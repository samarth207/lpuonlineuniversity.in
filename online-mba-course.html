FAQs

Frequently Asked
Questions

What is the Online MBA program?

The LPU Online MBA course provides basic management knowledge and gives a complete view of business across areas like marketing and finance, along with developing soft skills and leadership skills.

What is the eligibility criteria for Online MBA?

Graduation in any discipline or equivalent with a minimum of 50% marks (45% for reserved categories) from a recognized University. No entrance exam is required.

What are the job openings after completion of Online MBA program?

A variety of roles are available: Financial Analyst, Brand Manager, Human Resource Manager, Data Scientist, Digital Marketing Manager, and International Trade Specialist.

How is the curriculum designed in the Online MBA program?

LPU Online MBA program focuses on preparing students with advanced knowledge and skills in business management, leadership, and strategic decision-making.

Is an online MBA degree valid?

Yes, the LPU Online MBA is fully recognized. The degree is entitled by UGC - Distance Education Bureau and is also recognized by WES (World Education Services) for study in Canada and USA.

Will an online MBA be recognized for government positions?

Yes, the LPU Online MBA is valid for both private and government job applications. It holds the same credibility as a regular MBA.

Can I do a PhD after an Online MBA?

Yes, you can pursue a PhD after an Online MBA. Many universities accept online MBA graduates for doctoral programs based on eligibility criteria.

Is the Online MBA a full-time course?

The Online MBA is a flexible learning program, allowing students to study at their own pace while balancing work or other commitments.
